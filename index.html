Our story

A village library can open a world of possibility.

Metsanangwana is an afterschool programme for young people in Ga-Sekhukhune. The village library is a place to spend time learning, making things with your hands and discovering new ways to understand the world around you.

We bring together creative arts and crafts, environmental education and computer classes in a friendly, community-centred space.

Making the most of our village library.
